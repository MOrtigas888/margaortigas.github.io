About Marga Ortigas

Marga Ortigas is a journalist, author, and communications consultant whose work spans more than two decades across continents. She has covered conflict, climate emergencies, political upheaval, and human stories that reveal the quiet strength people carry through change.

As an author, Marga explores themes of identity, belonging, reinvention, and the emotional landscapes of migration and midlife. Her writing blends clarity, empathy, and a deep understanding of the human condition.

Today, she works with journalists, leaders, and storytellers to help them communicate with presence, gravitas, and authenticity — guiding them to craft narratives that resonate and endure.

She divides her time between Manila, Madrid, and wherever stories call.
